Empowering Innovation & Venture Creation

Established at Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna, CIMP Business Incubation & Innovation Foundation (CIMP-BIIF) is a Section 8 non-profit company dedicated to nurturing high-potential startups and building an inclusive entrepreneurial ecosystem.

01 Capital Access
Investor & Industry Network

Direct linkages with angel networks, venture capital syndicates, and seed investment channels.

02 Infrastructure
Co-Working & Lab Space

Plug-and-play modern workstations, high-speed connectivity, meeting suites, and lab access.

03 Legal & ROC
Company Incorporation

End-to-end guidance for legal entity structuring, ROC registration, GST, and compliances.

04 IP Protection
IPR & Patent Filing

Full-cycle intellectual property search, patent drafting, prior art review, and trademark filings.

05 Strategic Advice
Executive Mentorship

1-on-1 strategic guidance from 40+ seasoned domain veterans, corporate leaders, and founders.

06 Capacity Building
Hands-On Workshops

Masterclasses on product-market fit, unit economics, pitch deck prep, and go-to-market scaling.

07 Human Capital
Talent & Internship Link

Direct access to top management, analytics, and technical student talent for live projects.

08 Govt & CSR
Ecosystem Advisory

Fast-track liaison with Startup Bihar, Startup India, central ministries, and corporate CSR funds.

Startup Schemes & Innovation Opportunities

CIMP-BIIF facilitates access to seed funding grants, central incubation challenges, and institutional knowledge platforms for innovative startups and student entrepreneurs.

₹10L Seed Grant

Startup Bihar

Department of Industries, Govt. of Bihar
  • Seed grant assistance up to ₹10 Lakhs financial support.
  • Incubation endorsement & business plan evaluation by CIMP.
  • Subsidized physical workstation access at B-HUB & IPR reimbursement.
Open Startup Bihar Portal
₹15L Idea Grant

MSME Idea Hackathon

Ministry of MSME, Government of India
  • Non-dilutive grant funding up to ₹15 Lakhs per approved idea.
  • Prototype development, testing, and design labs facilitation.
  • Direct guidance from CIMP-BIIF as Host Institute (HI).
Open MSME Champions Portal
National Challenge

MoE IIC & Yukti Challenge

Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC)
  • Yukti Innovation Challenge: National repository & scaling track.
  • AICTE Productisation Fellowship: Student innovator stipend/grant.
  • Formal Institutional Innovation Council (IIC) idea evaluation by CIMP.

Our Key Focus Areas

CIMP-BIIF incubates ventures tackling pivotal societal, environmental, and technological challenges aligned with the United Nations SDGs.

SDG 01

End poverty in all its forms everywhere through inclusive entrepreneurship.

SDG 02

End hunger, achieve food security, improved nutrition, and sustainable agriculture.

SDG 03

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages via Health-Tech.

SDG 04

Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education through Edu-Tech innovations.

SDG 05

Achieve gender equality and empower women entrepreneurs across Bihar.

SDG 06

Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all.

SDG 07

Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy solutions.

SDG 08

Promote sustained, inclusive economic growth, productive employment and decent work.

SDG 09

Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive industrialization and foster innovation.

SDG 10

Reduce inequality within and among communities by providing venture access.

SDG 11

Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable.

SDG 12

Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns in early enterprises.

SDG 13

Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts via green startups.

SDG 14

Conserve and sustainably use oceans, seas and marine resources.

SDG 15

Protect, restore and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems.

SDG 16

Promote peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development and good governance.

SDG 17

Ecosystem alliances across industry, academia, government, and venture capital.
