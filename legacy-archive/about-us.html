CIMP Business Incubation and Innovation Foundation (CIMP-BIIF), stands as a beacon of entrepreneurial empowerment and innovation in North India. Established in 2021 with steadfast support from the Department of Industries, Government of Bihar, CIMP-BIIF embodies a commitment to fostering entrepreneurship, driving innovation, and propelling economic growth through the cultivation of startups.

Originally launched in 2017 as the Startup Incubation Centre (SIC), CIMP-BIIF underwent a transformative journey, evolving into a section 8 company and adopting its current name to better reflect its expanded scope and aspirations. This transition marked a significant milestone, solidifying CIMP-BIIF's dedication to providing world-class incubation facilities and support services to startups across Bihar.

CIMP-BIIF operates at the forefront of Bihar's burgeoning startup ecosystem, playing a pivotal role in shaping policies and initiatives aimed at catalyzing entrepreneurial growth. Collaborating closely with the Department of Industries, CIMP-BIIF actively contributed to the formulation and implementation of Bihar's startup policies, aligning its efforts with the state's vision of fostering innovation-driven economic development.

The foundation's focus spans a diverse array of sectors, including Fin-Tech, Edu-Tech, E-Commerce, Social Enterprise, and Health-Tech, reflecting its commitment to addressing contemporary challenges and driving transformative change across various industries.

At CIMP-BIIF, our ethos is rooted in collaboration, innovation, and impact. We provide comprehensive support to startups at every stage of their journey, offering robust business development assistance, facilitating access to government schemes and policies, fostering professional networks, and expediting investment opportunities. Through these initiatives, we empower budding entrepreneurs, equipping them with the resources, mentorship, and networks needed to scale their ventures and realize their full potential.

Driven by a vision to revolutionize Bihar's innovation ecosystem, CIMP-BIIF aspires to transform the state into a leading destination for startups in India. We envision a thriving startup community that drives economic growth, fuels innovation, and creates a better future for all stakeholders.

Our mission is to create a culture of innovation by empowering startups with impactful incubation, robust training, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and resourceful networks. We are committed to fostering and promoting entrepreneurship, helping startups become sustainable and scalable enterprises that contribute to Bihar's prosperity and progress.

In essence, CIMP-BIIF stands as a catalyst for change, a hub of innovation, and a driving force behind Bihar's journey towards becoming a vibrant hub for startups and innovation on the national stage. Through our collaborative efforts and unwavering commitment, we strive to create lasting impact, empower entrepreneurs, and build a brighter future for Bihar and beyond.
