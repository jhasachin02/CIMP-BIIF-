Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna (CIMP)

Enjoying a track-record of 100% placement of all its students since its inception, Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna, is not only one of the best PGDM institute in terms of placement, but is also a top ranked B-school in India in every parameter.

Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna (CIMP) stands as a beacon of excellence in the realm of management education in India. Established in 2008 with unwavering support from the Government of Bihar, CIMP has consistently garnered recognition for its outstanding contributions. Notably, it has earned a position among the top 125 management institutes in India as per the prestigious NIRF ranking of 2023, conducted by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Operating autonomously, CIMP holds the distinguished AAA+ rating from Career 360, affirming its stature as a premier business school. Within the governmental sphere, it proudly claims the 31st position among government B-schools and the 6th spot in the entire East Zone, a testament to its commitment to excellence.

At CIMP, students undergo rigorous training tailored to mold them into adept professional managers. The institute offers a comprehensive two-year full-time Post Graduate Diploma in Management, duly approved by AICTE, accredited by NBA, and recognized by AIU as equivalent to an MBA. Furthermore, CIMP extends its academic prowess with an AICTE-approved Fellow Programme in Management (FPM), catering to aspiring scholars.

Expanding its academic portfolio, CIMP has received AICTE's nod to introduce the Post Graduate Diploma in Management- Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Venture Development (PGDM-IEV), alongside the Executive Fellow Programme in Management (EFPM). These initiatives reflect CIMP's proactive approach in meeting the evolving demands of the business landscape.

Acknowledging the needs of working professionals, CIMP presents an eleven-month Executive Programme in Management, generously sponsored by the Government of Bihar, facilitating continuous learning and skill enhancement.

CIMP's sterling achievements resonate well in the corporate echelons, with its graduates consistently finding placements in esteemed national and international organizations. Through its unwavering dedication to academic excellence and holistic development, CIMP continues to shape the future leaders of the management realm.

Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna - Business Incubation & Innovation Foundation (CIMP-BIIF) brings together entrepreneurs, researchers, students, business leaders, and investors to foster a vibrant culture of innovation and venture building.
