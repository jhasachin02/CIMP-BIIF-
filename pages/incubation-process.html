Incubation Process

Structured Incubation & Acceleration Pathway

CIMP-BIIF offers a structured, phase-driven incubation ecosystem to nurture early-stage startups from concept ideation to market scale. Through specialized mentorship, institutional validation, and national grant linkages, founders receive end-to-end handholding.

Stage 01
Application & Screening

Startups apply via Startup Bihar or institutional incubation channels. Initial pitch deck review is conducted by the CIMP incubation committee.

Stage 02
Pre-Incubation (16 Wks)

Assigned institutional mentors refine the business plan, product ideation, unit economics, market benchmarking, and break-even projections.

Stage 03
Grant & Seed Approval

Final business plans are submitted to Department of Industries, GoB or central grant agencies (MSME / MoE) for seed funding approval.

Stage 04
Incubation & Scaling

Full acceleration support: prototype labs, co-working, commercialization, GTM strategy, investor pitching, and regulatory compliance.

Mentorship Governance & Allocation Framework

  • External as well as Internal faculty mentors are empanelled and matched to startups based on technical domain and sector requirements.
  • The updated CIMP Mentor directory is officially maintained and linked with the state database at startup.bihar.gov.in.
  • Mentors are assigned from the Expert Committee on Startups created in consultation with the Department of Industries, Government of Bihar.
  • A ceiling of 10 ongoing projects per mentor is strictly maintained to ensure high-touch, dedicated guidance for each founder.
  • Faculty mentors conduct quarterly progress reviews, unit financial assessments, and serve as single point of contact between CIMP-BIIF and the startup unit.
  • Regular group mentorship clinics are organized covering market linkages, investor relations, and banking connections.
