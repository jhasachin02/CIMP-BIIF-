Onboarding Gateway

Select your onboarding pathway below to initiate the registration sequence.

Incubation Cohort

FOR STARTUPS & EARLY-STAGE VENTURES

Apply for our structured incubation program to access seed recommendations, coworking spaces, milestone mentoring cycles, and institutional validation.

  • Eligibility for Bihar Startup Policy seed loan
  • Dedicated physical coworking desk at B-HUB Patna
  • Weekly progress milestones & advisory feedback

Advisory Panel

FOR MENTORS & DOMAIN EXPERTS

Join our network of experienced professionals, venture capitalists, and industry veterans to guide and advise the next generation of founders.

  • Conduct 1-on-1 startup advice office hours
  • Deliver specialized guest workshops & masterclasses
  • Participate in cohort screening & evaluation panels

Application Process

A simple 3-step sequence to get onboarded with CIMP-BIIF

1

Submit Form

Choose your pathway above and fill in the details in our quick digital application form.

2

Profile Review

Our incubation screening committee reviews your credentials and schedules a short online interaction.

3

Cohort Approval

Upon positive validation, you receive portal access credentials, coworking desk allocation, or mentor matching.

Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna - Business Incubation & Innovation Foundation (CIMP-BIIF) brings together entrepreneurs, researchers, students, business leaders, and investors to foster a vibrant culture of innovation and venture building.
